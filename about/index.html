About Sanja

I've spent 25 years on the delivery side of transformation.

I founded Marais Consulting in 2017 to do the work I enjoy most: helping leaders make big technology decisions well, then making sure those decisions actually land. I'm based in Brisbane and work with boards, executive teams and technology leaders across Australia and beyond.

How I got here

Technology, people and risk are never separate problems.

My career didn't start in a server room. In 2000 I co-founded a security company in South Africa and ran everything behind the scenes: operations, finance, HR, training and IT. We grew from six people to 750, from patrol officers to VIP protection teams. That taught me early that technology, people and risk are never separate problems. It's also why I trained as an organisational psychologist, and why I still look at every transformation through a people lens as well as a technical one.

When I moved to Australia in 2010, I started again as a project coordinator on a technology program and worked my way up. Most of the years since have been in healthcare: first at Medibank Health Solutions on Defence health contracts, then at Aspen Medical, where I led the technology and security function and served as Chief Technology & Security Officer.

At Aspen I co-designed and led the build of the national platform behind the Federal Government's COVID-19 respiratory clinics, which supported more than 6.8 million patient visits. I took the organisation through global ISO 27001:2022 certification and an IRAP assessment, built its AI roadmap, and founded Aspen Medical Technology, taking a digital health product from concept to market in two years.
